7 · Student accounts

Each student signs in with the email on the roster and a password. You create the accounts here — students cannot create their own. Every account starts with the password 123456; the student is asked to choose their own password the first time they sign in (they can change it once). If a student forgets their password, press reset to put it back to 123456.
